# Savior King — Hybrid Fighter (Neo × Shao Kahn × Beerus + SNK/MvC)

Boss-size crowned martial artist. Magic-series gatlings (MvC/SNK), destruction energy (Beerus), matrix slips (Neo), imperial shoulder/finishers (Shao Kahn).

## Normals / Combos (MvC + SNK)
- Stand/crouch/jump: LP MP HP / LK MK HK
- Gatlings on hit: LP→MP→HP, LK→MK→HK (ground + air)
- Special-cancel normals into Rush / Throne / Spin / Hakai Spark / Emperor Shoulder
- Super-cancel from normals/specials on hit (when meter allows)

Open in Fighter Factory 3
1. File > Open project files:
   SaviorKing.sff  SaviorKing.air  SaviorKing.def  SaviorKing.cns  SaviorKing.cmd  SaviorKing.snd
2. work/sprites/ contains PNG export named GROUP-INDEX.png for re-import.
3. Animations > Play to preview. Add CLSN 1 / CLSN 2 to tweak boxes.
4. Alignment tool: axis is at the feet of each sprite.
Index 0 of the palette is black (transparent).
